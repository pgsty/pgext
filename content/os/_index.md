Since 2026-11-21, active targets are **PostgreSQL 15–19** across **16 platforms** (8 distribution releases × 2 architectures). EL8 continues to serve **PostgreSQL 15–18** (`PG19` is `N/A`), while EL9/10, Debian 12/13, and Ubuntu 22.04/24.04/26.04 cover **PostgreSQL 15–19**. PG14 has retired from the active build targets. Consult the [availability matrix](/os/matrix/) for published packages.
