Four answers. **PGDG** means the extension comes from the official PostgreSQL Global Development Group repositories. **PIGSTY** means it is built here. **CONTRIB** means it ships inside the PostgreSQL source tree and needs no extra package at all. **MIXED** means the two repositories each carry part of the family.
